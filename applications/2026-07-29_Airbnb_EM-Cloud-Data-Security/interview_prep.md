# Interview Prep — Airbnb, Engineering Manager, Cloud & Data Security

## Role snapshot
- US - Remote Eligible (occasional office/offsite travel)
- Base salary: $212,000–$265,000 + bonus/equity/travel credits
- Charter: lead a team of security engineers securing cloud infrastructure, data platforms, and identity/access controls; own roadmap, people, and outcomes.

## Likely behavioral questions
1. You haven't held a formal management title — walk me through how you've led, mentored, and grown other engineers without one.
2. Tell me about a time you had to make an architectural security decision and communicate the risk tradeoffs to non-technical stakeholders or senior leadership.
3. Describe a time you acted as an embedded security partner rather than a gatekeeper — how did you get engineering teams to want to work with you?
4. Tell me about a time you had to build a "paved road" (automation, tooling, standard pattern) instead of manually reviewing the same thing repeatedly.
5. How do you approach coaching someone through a difficult performance conversation or career setback, even informally?
6. Describe your experience partnering with Infrastructure/Data Platform/Application Security-equivalent teams elsewhere in your career.
7. What's the hardest tradeoff you've made between shipping fast and shipping secure?

## Likely technical questions
1. Walk through your AWS/GCP IAM implementation work — policies, roles, and how you approached least-privilege at the scale you worked at.
2. How would you design access controls and data governance for a sensitive data pipeline (e.g., the SIEM/Elasticsearch ingestion work you did for DOE/NNSA)?
3. What's your experience with Kubernetes and container security specifically — be honest that this is platform-user-level exposure, not cluster administration or K8s security architecture ownership.
4. How do you think about cloud-native detection and prevention controls — talk through your detection quality metrics and staged-rollout practice.
5. How would you approach securing a multi-cloud environment given your AWS/GCP background (note: Azure exposure is API-orchestration only, via Sentinel/Defender, not deep Azure-native architecture).
6. Describe the API token/role/permission management work across your nine-SIEM orchestration framework — how did you avoid overprivileged tokens?

## Sharp questions to ask them
1. Is this role expected to build the team from where it stands today, or is there an existing team with an established roadmap I'd be inheriting?
2. How does Cloud & Data Security currently partner with Infrastructure and Data Platform day-to-day — is that relationship already strong, or part of what this role needs to build?
3. What does "shift security left through paved roads and automation" look like concretely on this team today?
4. How is success measured for this role in the first 6–12 months?
5. What's the team's current size, and what's the growth plan tied to this hire?

## Salary anchor
Posted range is $212,000–$265,000. Anchor to the top third (~$250K+) once fit is confirmed, well above Kyle's $170K+ floor.

## Closing-the-interview script
"I want to be upfront that I haven't held a formal people-management title, but I've spent years directing technical priorities and mentoring detection engineers as a technical lead — what I'm missing in title, I think I make up for in the technical depth and partnership instincts this role needs. What would make you confident that trade-off works for this team?"
