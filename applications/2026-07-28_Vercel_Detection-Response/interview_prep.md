# Interview Prep — Vercel, Security Engineer, Detection Response

## Likely behavioral questions
1. Tell me about a time you improved visibility/logging infrastructure to catch something you were missing.
2. Describe supporting a compliance audit (SOC2/PCI/ISO) — even if from the detection/evidence-gathering side rather than owning the GRC process.
3. Tell me about handling an urgent security incident with shifting priorities.
4. Describe a time you managed a key security vendor/partner relationship.
5. Tell me about working in a fast-paced environment with frequent priority shifts.

## Likely technical questions
1. Walk through your SIEM management experience — how do you structure detection content for a small, fast-moving security team?
2. Your production coding is Python, not Rust or Go — how would you ramp on a compiled language quickly if the team needs it?
3. Describe using SQL for a security investigation — walk through a real example.
4. How would you approach GitHub administration and security controls for an engineering-heavy org, even without direct prior GitHub-admin experience?
5. Walk through your incident response process from detection through triage to remediation.
6. How do you think about endpoint security posture (EDR, configuration) across Mac/Windows/Linux?

## Questions to ask them
1. Since I'm not within commuting distance of SF/NY/London/Berlin, can you confirm this is a fully remote role for my location?
2. What does the managed SOC relationship look like, and how much of the alert volume do they handle vs. your team?
3. How is work split between CorpSec, detection engineering, and compliance-audit support day to day?
4. What's the current state of the on-call rotation and process?
5. What tooling gaps is the team actively trying to close right now?

## Salary anchor
Posted range: **$208,000–$312,000** (SF band). Well above your $170K floor. Anchor to the top third once fit and remote status are both confirmed.

## Closing-the-interview script
"I'd love to bring that SIEM and incident-response background to Vercel's Detection Response team — what does the roadmap look like for the next year, and is there anything about my background you'd like me to expand on?"
