# Interview Prep — OpenAI, Security Engineer, Insider Threat Detection & Response

## Likely behavioral questions
1. Tell me about a time you investigated anomalous user/entity behavior and had to determine if it was malicious or benign.
2. Describe a time you had to work cross-functionally with a non-technical stakeholder (HR, Legal, compliance) on a sensitive matter. (Be honest if this is thin — pivot to cross-functional technical partnerships you have led.)
3. Tell me about a time you balanced user friction against risk reduction in a security control.
4. Describe how you've used AI to accelerate an investigation or reduce manual toil.
5. Tell me about a time you had to independently drive a project with ambiguous scope.
6. Describe a time your detection missed something, and how you adjusted afterward.

## Likely technical questions
1. Walk through how you'd design detections for data exfiltration via unusual channels (personal cloud storage, USB, etc.).
2. How would you build a baseline of "normal" user behavior to catch deviations, given your time-series anomaly work?
3. What's your experience with macOS/Windows/Linux endpoint telemetry specifically?
4. How do you tune detections to reduce user friction while still catching meaningful risk?
5. Walk through your GenAI tooling for false-positive triage — how would you adapt that to insider-risk signals specifically?
6. How do you approach an investigation where the "attacker" might be a legitimate employee acting within their access?

## Honest framing for the real gap
This role's core differentiator from general detection engineering is formal insider-risk investigations partnered with HR/Legal — genuinely new territory for Kyle. In the interview, be upfront: frame it as "I've built the detection and behavioral-analytics foundation this kind of program runs on, and I'm energized to learn the HR/Legal partnership and case-management side" rather than overclaiming prior insider-risk-specific casework.

## Sharp questions to ask them
1. How is the insider-risk program structured today — is there already a case-management workflow with HR/Legal, or is this role building that from scratch?
2. What does an escalation path look like when a detection surfaces something that needs Legal involvement?
3. How do you balance monitoring depth against employee trust/privacy at OpenAI specifically?
4. What's the biggest current gap in insider-threat detection coverage that this role is meant to close?

## Salary anchor
Posted range $230K–$385K — wide band, likely reflecting mid vs. senior level. Given real experience gap on the insider-risk-specific angle, anchor initial conversation around the middle-to-upper third ($290K–$330K) rather than the very top, and let technical depth in the screen justify pushing higher.

## Closing script
"The detection-engineering and behavioral-analytics foundation I've built over the last decade is a strong base for insider-risk work, and I'm genuinely excited to grow into the HR/Legal partnership side of this role. I'd love to hear more about how the team currently structures that collaboration."
