# Interview Prep — HubSpot, Principal Software Engineer, Security, Detection & Response

## Likely behavioral questions
1. Tell me about a time you acted as the technical lead/mentor for detection engineering work across a team.
2. Describe influencing architecture decisions for security logging infrastructure.
3. Tell me about leading incident response under real pressure (Treasury SOC context).
4. Describe a time you had to communicate a complex threat landscape to a non-technical audience.
5. Tell me about a coverage gap you identified and how you prioritized closing it.
6. Describe partnering with legal/privacy/product teams on incident response standards.
7. Tell me about the hardest engineering trade-off you made building detection automation.

## Likely technical questions
1. This role emphasizes production software engineering ownership more than content/analytics — how would you describe your engineering depth vs. detection-content depth? (Be honest — this is a real stretch point.)
2. Walk through correlating identity, cloud, and network telemetry to detect post-entry behavior.
3. How do you manage and ingest IOCs, and how would you approach STIX/TAXII if new to it?
4. Describe your GitLab CI/CD detection-as-code pipeline in depth.
5. How would you lead a high-severity CritSit using NIST 800-61/SANS methodology?
6. Walk through your GenAI-powered triage tooling — architecture and measured impact.
7. What's your experience with CrowdStrike Falcon specifically vs. ingesting its data downstream?

## Questions to ask them
1. How is "Principal" scoped here — more architecture/technical leadership, or hands-on production code ownership?
2. What does the in-person onboarding and summit cadence actually look like for a remote hire?
3. What's the current state of the detection-in-depth roadmap this role would inherit?
4. How does this role interface with Legal/Privacy on incident response standards day to day?
5. What would success look like in the first 90 days?

## Salary anchor
Posted range: **$266,200–$425,900.** Well above the $170K floor — anchor to the top third once fit is confirmed, but be ready to discuss the Principal-level scope honestly given the real stretch on pure software-engineering ownership.

## Closing-the-interview script
"I bring deep detection-content and analytics leadership plus real production delivery through CI/CD — I want to be upfront that my depth leans more detection/analytics than pure software engineering, so I'd love to hear how you're weighing that mix for this specific opening."
