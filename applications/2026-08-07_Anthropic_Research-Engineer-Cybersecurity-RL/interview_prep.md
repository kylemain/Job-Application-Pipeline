# Interview Prep — Research Engineer, Cybersecurity RL (Anthropic)

## Likely behavioral questions
1. Tell me about a time you applied AI/ML tooling to a real defensive-security problem — what worked, what didn't?
2. Describe a time you had to validate whether a model or automated system's output was actually correct before trusting it in production.
3. Walk me through building the Cysiv detection rules engine from scratch — how did you scope it and know it was working?
4. Tell me about a time you had to learn a new technical domain quickly to be useful on a project.
5. Describe how you've balanced "good enough to ship" against "worth investing more research time in."
6. Tell me about a disagreement with a teammate about a technical approach and how it resolved.
7. What draws you to Anthropic's mission specifically, versus staying in applied detection engineering?

## Likely technical questions
1. Walk through how you'd approach building a detection rule for a specific vulnerability class end to end.
2. How do you evaluate whether a piece of GenAI-generated detection content is actually good before deploying it?
3. What's your experience with vulnerability data (e.g., Tenable) and how have you turned it into detection content?
4. If you had to learn RL environment design from scratch, how would you approach ramping up quickly?
5. How do you think about the difference between a model that's technically correct and one that's safe to deploy in a security context?
6. Describe your GenAI-powered rule-translation tooling — how did you validate its output was reliable across different SIEM syntaxes?
7. What's your intuition for how an LLM might get vulnerability remediation wrong, based on your detection-engineering experience?

## Questions to ask them
1. What does the split between research exploration and production engineering actually look like week to week on this team?
2. How much ramp-up time is typical for someone strong in applied security but newer to RL specifically?
3. What does the Cybersecurity RL team's current biggest technical challenge look like?
4. How does this team collaborate with security specialists outside Anthropic, as mentioned in the posting?
5. What does success look like in the first 6 months for someone coming from an applied-security background rather than an ML-research background?

## Salary anchor
Posted band is $300,000-$405,000. Given the real gap on hands-on RL experience, this is a role where leveling and comp will likely track closely to interview performance on the RL/ML side — don't over-anchor to the top of the band before establishing how the team is thinking about your starting level.

## Closing-the-interview script
"I've spent my career on the applied defensive side — building detection content and, more recently, using GenAI directly in that work. I don't have formal RL research experience, but I bring real security domain depth and a track record of getting practical value out of AI tooling in production. I'd love to find out where that's most useful to this team. What's a good next step?"
