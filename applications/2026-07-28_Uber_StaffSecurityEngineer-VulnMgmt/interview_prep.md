# Interview Prep — Uber, Staff Security Engineer - Vulnerability Management

## Likely behavioral questions
1. Tell me about a time you turned a raw, noisy data source into something a team could actually act on.
2. Describe a time you had to convince a team to adopt a "shift-left" security practice earlier in their workflow.
3. Walk through mentoring a colleague or leading a technical initiative without formal authority.
4. Tell me about a time you had to make a risk-acceptance or false-positive call under uncertainty.
5. Describe a time you automated a manual, repetitive security workflow.

## Likely technical questions
1. Walk through how you ingested Tenable vulnerability scan data into an analytics platform — what did the resulting detection content actually do?
2. How would you design a unified, risk-scored view of vulnerabilities across on-prem, cloud, containers, and endpoints, drawing on your multi-SIEM orchestration experience?
3. Describe your GitLab CI/CD pipeline work — how would you integrate vulnerability scanning directly into that pipeline for a "shift-left" workflow?
4. How have you used GenAI for triage, and how would that extend to automated remediation-instruction generation for service owners?
5. You don't have confirmed RBVM framework or EASM/SSCS experience — how would you approach ramping up on risk-based prioritization logic specifically?
6. Walk through your Docker experience — how comfortable are you extending that into container vulnerability scanning and hardening?

## Questions to ask them
1. What does the current Risk-Based Vulnerability Management (RBVM) stack look like today, and what's the biggest gap this hire would close?
2. How is "Continuous Threat Exposure Management" currently operationalized on this team versus where you want it to go?
3. What does the split look like between building the platform itself versus doing hands-on vulnerability analysis/triage?
4. How does this team partner with Compliance and IT on vulnerability SLAs and exception processes?
5. What does success look like for this hire in the first 90 days?

## Salary anchor
Posted range: **$232,000–$258,000/year** base (same across Seattle/Sunnyvale/SF/NYC), plus bonus/equity — same band as the D&R role already applied to. This is the best level match of the three new Uber roles; anchor expectations to the middle of the band given strong overlap on core requirements.

## Closing-the-interview script
"I'd love to bring that vulnerability data engineering and multi-platform automation background to the RBVM platform — what would the first project look like for someone strong on the data/detection side of vulnerability management, ramping up on formal RBVM framework design?"
