# Interview Prep — HubSpot, Principal Software Engineer, Security, Detection & Response

## Likely behavioral questions
1. Tell me about leading a high-severity incident (CritSit) end to end.
2. Describe a time you influenced technical direction for a detection/response system beyond your own individual work.
3. Tell me about mentoring engineers on detection reliability or design review.
4. Describe translating a threat landscape into a prioritized detection-coverage roadmap.
5. Tell me about a time you had to explain a complex threat/incident to a non-technical audience.

## Likely technical questions
1. Walk through architecting a corporate security logging infrastructure/SIEM at scale — what were the key design tradeoffs?
2. Your IOC/threat-intel work hasn't been explicitly framed against STIX/TAXII — how would you map actor techniques to those standards quickly?
3. How do you correlate identity, cloud, and network telemetry to catch post-entry behavior rather than just initial-access signatures?
4. Describe your GenAI-powered detection automation — how do you validate it doesn't introduce blind spots?
5. This role expects deep hands-on software engineering ownership of detection systems — walk through the most complex system you personally built and shipped end to end.
6. How do you evaluate detection-as-code architecture decisions across a large-scale platform (build vs. buy, in-house pipeline vs. vendor)?
7. You don't currently hold GCIH/GCFA/CISSP — how do you stay current on incident-response methodology without them?

## Questions to ask them
1. What does "Principal" mean concretely on this team — IC technical leadership, org-wide architecture ownership, or both?
2. How is the detection engineering team structured relative to the broader security org?
3. What's the current state of AI/LLM tooling in detection and response here?
4. How is success measured for this role in the first 6 months?
5. What does the onboarding/in-person expectation actually look like for a fully remote hire?

## Salary anchor
Posted range: **$266,200–$425,900** — the highest of this batch, well above your $170K floor. Anchor to the top third once fit is confirmed, but expect the seniority bar (10-15 yrs, Principal scope) to be tested hard in the loop — be ready to speak concretely to the scale/impact of what you've personally built and shipped.

## Closing-the-interview script
"I'm excited about the scale of impact this role has on HubSpot's detection and response posture — what does the roadmap look like for the next year, and is there anything about my background you'd like me to expand on?"
