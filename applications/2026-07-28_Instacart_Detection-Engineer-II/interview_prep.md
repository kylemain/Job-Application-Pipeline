# Interview Prep — Instacart, Detection Engineer II

## Likely behavioral questions
1. Tell me about a time you scaled detection coverage faster than the team could hand-write rules.
2. Describe mentoring another engineer on detection logic or investigation technique.
3. Tell me about a detection you shipped that materially reduced analyst toil.
4. Describe partnering with Red Team or Incident Response to validate a detection.
5. Tell me about a time your detection-as-code pipeline caught a bad rule before it shipped.
6. Describe balancing detection coverage against log ingestion cost.
7. Tell me about the hardest forensic investigation you supported.

## Likely technical questions
1. Walk through your GitLab CI/CD pipeline for detection-as-code — what runs at each stage?
2. How would you approach building macOS-specific detection given your primary background is Windows/Linux/cloud telemetry? (Honest gap — be ready to talk learning plan, not false confidence.)
3. Describe your approach to reducing false positives across a high-volume SOAR pipeline.
4. Walk through building UEBA/ML-based detection content — what features mattered most?
5. How do you correlate identity, cloud, and container telemetry to catch post-entry behavior?
6. Describe your Python-based automation for detection engineering — what did you build vs. use off the shelf?
7. How do you approach detection coverage against zero-trust/token-theft attack paths?

## Questions to ask them
1. What does the current split look like between hand-written detections and automated/ML-assisted coverage?
2. How is the location-tiered pay band determined for a remote hire — is there room to discuss given the skills match?
3. What's the biggest gap in detection coverage the team is trying to close in the next two quarters?
4. How does Detection Engineering work with Red Team and Fraud/Trust & Safety day to day?
5. What does success look like for this role at the 6-month mark?

## Salary anchor
Posted range for Kyle's TX location: **$157,000–$165,500 — below the $170K floor.** This is worth raising directly and early: the CA/NY/CT/NJ band ($171,000–$180,500) clears the floor, so there may be room to discuss location-based banding, especially given the strength of the skills match. Don't proceed past a first conversation without addressing this.

## Closing-the-interview script
"This is exactly the kind of detection-as-code environment I've built my career around — I do want to be upfront that the posted band for my location is below where I need to land. Is there flexibility there, or a path to the higher band given the scope of experience?"
