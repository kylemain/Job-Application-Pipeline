# Interview Prep — Quora, Detection & CorpSec Engineer (Remote)

## Likely behavioral questions
1. Tell me about a time you built something because a commercial tool didn't fit the problem.
2. Describe advising a non-technical team on a security risk they hadn't considered.
3. Tell me about investigating a security incident from first alert through root cause.
4. Describe working on a small, high-ownership team where you owned a domain end to end.
5. Tell me about using AI tooling to make a security workflow faster or more scalable.

## Likely technical questions
1. Walk through building and maintaining a SIEM from scratch — what did you prioritize first?
2. Describe your most complex incident investigation — how did you go from log review to timeline reconstruction to root cause?
3. Your device-fleet/identity/Zero-Trust VPN experience is limited — how would you approach standing up corporate security controls for an employee fleet you haven't administered before?
4. How would you design canary tokens / early-warning mechanisms for critical assets?
5. Walk through a production Python detection pipeline you built and shipped alongside other engineers — what did code review look like?
6. How do you think about balancing production-side detection coverage with corporate security advisory work for non-engineering teams?

## Questions to ask them
1. How is the "genuinely hybrid" split between detection and corpsec work actually divided week to week?
2. What does the current identity/Zero-Trust VPN stack look like, and how far along is that PoC?
3. What commercial tools has the team decided *not* to buy, and why build in-house instead?
4. How does the team use AI coding tools in practice today?
5. What does success look like in the first 90 days for this role?

## Salary anchor
Posted range: **$172,279–$249,640** (US). Above your $170K floor. Anchor to the top third once fit is confirmed, factoring in that the corpsec half of the role is a genuine stretch — worth having a clear-eyed read on ramp time before final negotiation.

## Closing-the-interview script
"I'd love to bring that SIEM-building and investigation background to a team this hands-on — what does the roadmap look like for the corpsec side of the role, and is there anything about my background you'd like me to expand on?"
